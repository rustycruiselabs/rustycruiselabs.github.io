Coloring Mochi

About ColoringMochi

ColoringMochi is a free collection of printable coloring pages for all ages. Whether you’re a parent looking for fun activities for kids, a teacher preparing classroom resources, or an adult who enjoys the calming experience of coloring, you’ll find a variety of designs to explore.

This project began as a personal collection of coloring pages created for my nephew and niece to enjoy. It has since grown into a free resource shared with everyone.

All coloring pages on ColoringMochi are created using AI-assisted illustration tools and carefully refined to produce clean, print-ready black-and-white line art suitable for coloring.

All pages are free to download and print for personal use, classrooms, and creative projects. If you believe any content should be removed, please feel free to contact us.

Interactive World (Coming Soon)

In addition to printable pages, ColoringMochi also features an interactive browser-based coloring experience powered by a Unity WebGL game.

Users can upload their completed drawings and bring them into a simple digital world, where their creations become part of a playful, evolving environment. This feature is designed to extend the joy of coloring beyond paper—turning finished artwork into something interactive and shareable.

This idea was inspired by a visit to Arte Kids Park in Jeju, South Korea, where colored drawings can be scanned and projected onto a large screen, bringing them to life in a vibrant, interactive space. It was an incredibly fun experience that I enjoyed even as an adult. Hence, I wanted to recreate the experience here in Coloring Mochi.

This feature is still a work in progress, and I’m excited to continue building it into something magical for everyone to enjoy.
